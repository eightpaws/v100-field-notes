**Result.** Capture of CUDA graphs for ACE-Step 1.5 won't yield significant gain. Measurements via music studio API on xl-sft with LM 1.7B: | Duration | Steps | Seconds | |---|---|---| | 30s | 8 | 72.2 | | 30s | 32 | 87.3 | | 60s | 8 | 90.7 | | 60s | 32 | 118.6 | Hence, the breakdown: adding 24 steps costs 15.1s, i.e., **0.63s per diffusion step**; adding 30s of audio - 18.5s. The remaining 93% is language model code planning and audio decoding, where CUDA graphs aren't applied: they have variable length and autoregression. Conclusion: this direction is closed as low-value. If speeding up music, then not with graphs, but by reducing LM work (fewer planning steps, prompt caching, using 0.6B model instead of 1.7B) and decoder. V100-25 card responds "no": expected gain within noise. Side note: Ollama held 10.3GB and caused generation failure with OOM; for music and video tasks, it should be stopped to prevent studio from starting.


**Verdict.** Capture of CUDA graphs for ACE-Step 1.5 won't yield significant gain.
