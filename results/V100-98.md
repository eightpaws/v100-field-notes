# V100-98 — Llama.cpp RPC: one model on two cards

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** `rpc-server` in llama.cpp enables combining V100 (16 GB) and 4060 Ti (16 GB) into a single pool without vLLM. It's an alternative to V100-31 (branch `volta-ada` in 1Cat) and a way to verify if 32 GB total memory at 77 MB/s bandwidth is feasible.

**Environment.** Guest AI-GPU on local stand: Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB, cc 7.0, driver 580.178.04, CUDA-toolkit 12.9 (binaries from release b12811 compiled for cuda 12.8, sm_70 works); second card - RTX 4060 Ti 16GB, cc 8.9, driver 617.14, Windows, `ggml-rpc-server` from the same release b12811 (RPC protocol v10.0.2), channel between machines - HTTP-test 106 MB/s (370 MB in 3.5s).

**Commands.** `./llama-bench -m /models/qwen3-30b-a3b.gguf -p 512 -n 128 -r 3 -ngl 99 --rpc <second-card>:50052 -dev CUDA0/RPC0 -ts 0.50/0.50`; base line: `./llama-bench -m /models/qwen3-30b-a3b.gguf -p 512 -n 128 -r 3 -ngl 99 -ncmoe 8 -dev CUDA0`. On the other side: `ggml-rpc-server.exe -H 0.0.0.0 -p 50052 -c`.

**Artifacts.** /root/runs/V100-98/run.log (base line, 1486 B) and /root/runs/V100-98b/run.log (split, 944 B) on the guest; publication - [raw/runs/V100-98.json](../raw/runs/V100-98.json).

**Repeats.** n=3 (flag `-r 3` for llama-bench), provided are mean ± stddev across three runs of each configuration.

**Result.**

**Verdict.** 27.11.2026, llama.cpp b12811 (00d5c142c, ready binaries ubuntu-cuda-12.8), Qwen3-30B-A3B Q4_K_M 17.28 GB (does not fit in 16 GB of one card).

**Result type.** Mixed.

**Does not show.** the run log was not preserved, so the numbers cannot be re-checked from this repository; no second engine or model was measured on the same harness.

 27.11.2026, llama.cpp b12811 (00d5c142c, ready binaries ubuntu-cuda-12.8), Qwen3-30B-A3B Q4_K_M 17.28 GB (does not fit in 16 GB of one card). Split V100 + 4060 Ti via `--rpc` (`-dev CUDA0/RPC0 -ts 0.50/0.50`): pp512 **2405 ± 136 tok/s**, tg128 **54.9 ± 3.7 tok/s** (n=3). Base line - same model on one V100 only way possible, `-ncmoe 8` (experts 8 layers in RAM): pp512 **1181 ± 36**, tg128 **77.9 ± 12.6 tok/s** (n=3). Conclusion: pool of two cards gives ×2.0 prefill, but decode is 29% slower than CPU experts on one card; channel 106 MB/s not bottleneck for decode - eaten by RPC overhead per layer.
