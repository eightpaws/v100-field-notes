# V100-56 — 32kB Q8_0 and check Q4_0 at 64k

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** Q4_0 KV is half of Q8_0 and can provide 64k context for 14B where Q8_0 does not fit.

**Environment.** llama.cpp, Qwen2.5-Coder-7B FA, KV q8_0 at 32k and q4_0 at 64k; V100 16 GB (sm_70), driver 580, CUDA 12.9

**Commands.** The run script was not preserved; the runner was `/root/bin/v100-run.sh V100-NN`.

**Artifacts.** Logs were not preserved for this run.

**Repeats.** single run

**Result.** 32k and 64k are achievable, decoding is unaffected.** Qwen2.5-Coder-7B Q4 (4.36 GiB), FA enabled: | Context | KV | Prefill | Decode | |---|---|---|---| | 32,768 | q8_0 | **1860 tok/s** | 121.5 tok/s | | 65,536 | q4_0 | **1247 tok/s** | 121.8 tok/s | Both configurations load and run: 64k on q4_0 avoids layer eviction, decode speed is the same as short context (121-122 tok/s), prefill drops linearly (1860 → 1247). Thus, for files of 50-60 thousand tokens, the 7B model on this card is suitable if KV is kept in q4_0. Compressed KV is mandatory: 64k in f16 on 16 GB does not fit.
