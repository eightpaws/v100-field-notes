# V100-103 — Batch savings: Batch vs individual loads

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Result type.** Positive.

**Question.** Switching models takes time: 7B - 3-4 s, DCV2-Lite 16B - around 10 s, Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B - 40-60 s (our measurements on V100-39 and V100-63). If running cards by number rather than model, reload time exceeds measurement time. No fixed savings figures - only estimate.

**Environment.** llama.cpp build 12359 (commit b1b770d90), CUDA build for sm_70, driver 580, guest the GPU guest on home stand (V100 16 GB), warm page cache (89 GiB), foreign processes on card removed v100-clean.sh.

**Repeats.** Single run on configuration; warm-up load spread within series: 7B 1.51-1.51-1.51-2.55 s, 30B 3.53-3.57 s. Cold start taken once (first in series).

**Result.** Group of four runs on one model: batch 23.9 s vs. variance 39.1 s, savings 38.8%. Warm switching: 7B 1.5 s, 16B 2.5 s, 30B 3.5 s, i.e., 10 times less than the 40-60 s figure from the card (which was cold). Cold first load: 7B 1.8 s, 16B 12.2 s, 30B 4.4 s; on 16B batch lost (12.9 vs. 11.0 s) due to cold first load. Conclusion: grouping cards by model is beneficial, savings 58-69% on 7B and 30B. VRAM: 4.9 / 11.1 / 12.6 GB.
