# V100-17 — LMDeploy TurboMind: AWQ without forks

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** TurboMind is the only way where sm_70 is not a fork, but the official goal: AWQ/GPTQ int4-cores with tensor `8x8x4` WMMA. If it works, we get a stable 4-bit server without building foreign branches and without the risk that everything will collapse tomorrow.

**Result.** Partial: TurboMind works on sm_70 but loses quality in 4-bit. Speed: decode 131.2 tok/s (n=3, stdev 0.13), prefill 4266 tok/s at 751 token, VRAM 14.8 GiB, warm-up 50s. Quality: MBPP pass@1 0.50 (20/40) vs 0.625 of Qwen2.5-Coder-7B in Q8_0 - eight tasks out of forty are lost in 4-bit. Conclusion: TurboMind is suitable as a fast server but not as a replacement for Q8 where code quality matters.
