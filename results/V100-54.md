With cuDNN disabled a 1024x1024 fp16 frame does not fit: OOM for 4,5 GiB with 13,35 GiB already allocated, so the frame needs tiling or 768 px.
**Verdict.** Mixed, and the reason is the missing cuDNN path on sm_70, not the model or the size of the card.
