# V100-157 — Gemma 4 26B-A4B: 32k context and quality on long prompt

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** V100-145 closed 8k (15 331 MiB, 124 tok/s decode); it grows to four times on 32k KV-cache, and the question is whether it fits with q8_0 KV and what happens to the quality and speed at that time.

**Environment.** Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB (sm_70), driver 580.178.04, CUDA 12.9, llama.cpp 28148b5 (build 1, CUDA sm_70), Ubuntu 24.04.5, kernel 6.8.0-151-generic, Guest VM on home bench.

**Commands.** `llama-server -m /models/strata/gemma4/gemma-4-26B_q4_0-it.gguf -ngl 99 -c 32768 -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0 -t 8 --jinja --host 127.0.0.1 --port 18497`

**Artifacts.** /root/runs/V100-157/run.log, /root/runs/V100-157b/run.log, /root/runs/V100-157c/run.log; numbers - /srv/media/night/v100_157.json, v100_157b.json, v100_157c.json.

**Repeats.** Decoded n=3 (mean ± std dev); Prefilled on a long prompt and search for a needle - single run.

**Result.**

**Verdict.** 32k context with q8_0 KV fits: rise 34s, peak VRAM 15,217 MiB of 16,144 (reserve 927 MiB).
