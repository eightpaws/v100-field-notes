# V100-73 — Goods cards per hour: image generation throughput

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** All measurements V100 are dedicated to text, but the money comes from the image: how many product photos per hour does the card produce with SDXL and with Flux in fp8.

**Result.** On a 768 px card, it yields ~2100 images per hour, but on 1024 px it hits memory.** Tested on DreamShaperXL-Turbo (6 steps, guidance 1.5, fp16) via diffusers, local single-file checkpoint: | Size | Seconds per image | Images per hour | Peak VRAM | |---|---|---|---| | 512×512 | **1.1** | **3273** | 9.9 GB | | 768×768 | **1.7** | **2118** | 13.7 GB | | 1024×1024 | OOM when allocating 4.5 GB | — | — | Gotchas that prevent anything from running: cuDNN 9 on sm_70 can't find an engine for convolutions (`GET was unable to find an engine to execute this computation`) - both on HF-SDXL repo and local checkpoints. Fixed by `torch.backends.cudnn.enabled = False`; afterwards, 512 and 768 work fine, but 1024 falls due to memory as without cuDNN convolutions are computed via im2col and require four times more VRAM. VAE in fp32 doesn't work - latent fp16 results in `Input type (c10::Half) and bias type (float)`; VAE remains fp16, slicing and tiling are enabled. Practical conclusion: for commercial cards, 768 px is the working mode (35 cards per minute), while 1024 px and printing require either fixing cuDNN for Volta or upscaling from 768 (V100-06). **Unable to download official checkpoint - HF limits output.** Tries: full load via `curl` gets stuck at 8-16 KB, range download passes up to 3-4 GB and fails, once reached 18.24 GB but with extra 7 KB at the end and failed to load (`failed to load model` even after trimming to exact size). Around 60 GB downloaded from this IP in a day, then server started giving 8 KB per request. Background repeats left (`/srv/media/night/glm_loop.log`, up to 60 cycles of 5 minutes each). Once the file is complete, harness run will take 20 minutes, weights are already prepared.
