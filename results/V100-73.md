# V100-73 — Goods cards per hour: image generation throughput

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** All measurements V100 are dedicated to text, but the money comes from the image: how many product photos per hour does the card produce with SDXL and with Flux in fp8.

**Environment.** Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB, sm_70, driver 580; torch 2.10.0+cu128, diffusers 0.39.0, venv `/var/lib/ollama/venvs/video`; cuDNN disabled (`torch.backends.cudnn.enabled = False`); checkpoint `/models/image/DreamShaperXL_Turbo_v2_1.safetensors` (single-file, fp16), 6 steps, guidance 1.5, VAE fp16 with slicing and tiling.

**Commands.** `python3 /home/hermes/v100_73d_images.py`; /root/bin/v100-run.sh; /root/runs/V100-73/; pid.txt. (Russian note in the field notes is omitted from this English export.)

**Artifacts.** /srv/media/night/v100_73g_run.log; /srv/media/generated/images/v73_turbo_512.jpg; v73_turbo_768.jpg; /srv/media/night/v100_73.json. (Russian note in the field notes is omitted from this English export.)

**Repeats.** Single run, one frame per size; the images-per-hour column is an extrapolation of 3600 / 1.7 s from a single measurement, so spread was not measured and 2118 is an upper estimate rather than an average.

**Results.**

| Size | Seconds per image | Images per hour | Peak VRAM |
|---|---|---|---|
| 512x512 | **1.1** | **3273** | 9.9 GB |
| 768x768 | **1.7** | **2118** | 13.7 GB |
| 1024x1024 | OOM on allocating 4.5 GB | - | - |

**Result.** At 768 px the card renders one image in 1.7 s, about 2100 per hour; at 1024 px it runs out of memory. Measured on DreamShaperXL-Turbo (6 steps, guidance 1.5, fp16) through diffusers from a local single-file checkpoint. Two gotchas decide whether anything runs at all: cuDNN 9 on sm_70 cannot find an engine for convolutions (`GET was unable to find an engine to execute this computation`), on the HF SDXL repo and on local checkpoints alike, which is fixed by `torch.backends.cudnn.enabled = False`; after that 512 and 768 work, but 1024 falls out of memory because without cuDNN convolutions go through im2col and need about four times the VRAM. The VAE cannot run in fp32 either: latent fp16 raises `Input type (c10::Half) and bias type (float)`, so the VAE stays fp16 with slicing and tiling enabled.

**Verdict.** For commercial cards, 768 px is the working mode (35 cards per minute), while 1024 px and printing require either fixing cuDNN for Volta or upscaling from 768 (V100-06).
