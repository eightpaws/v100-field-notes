# V100-73 — Goods cards per hour: image generation throughput

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** All measurements V100 are dedicated to text, but the money comes from the image: how many product photos per hour does the card produce with SDXL and with Flux in fp8.

**Environment.** Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB, sm_70, driver 580; torch 2.10.0+cu128, diffusers 0.39.0, venv `/var/lib/ollama/venvs/video`; cuDNN disabled (`torch.backends.cudnn.enabled = False`); checkpoint `/models/image/DreamShaperXL_Turbo_v2_1.safetensors` (single-file, fp16), 6 steps, guidance 1.5, VAE fp16 with slicing and tiling.

**Commands.** `python3 /home/hermes/v100_73d_images.py`; /root/bin/v100-run.sh; /root/runs/V100-73/; pid.txt. (Russian note in the field notes is omitted from this English export.)

**Artifacts.** /srv/media/night/v100_73g_run.log; /srv/media/generated/images/v73_turbo_512.jpg; v73_turbo_768.jpg; /srv/media/night/v100_73.json. (Russian note in the field notes is omitted from this English export.)

**Repeats.** Single run, one frame per size; "images per hour" is an extrapolation of 3600 / 1.7s from a single measurement, spread was not measured. The figure 2118 is an upper estimate, not the average. **Result:** **At 768 px, the model yields ~2100 images per hour, at 1024 px it runs out of memory.** Measured on DreamShaperXL-Turbo (6 steps, guidance 1.5, fp16) via diffusers, local single-file checkpoint: | Size | Seconds per image | Images per hour | Peak VRAM | |---|---|---|---| | 512×512 | **1.1** | **3273** | 9.9 GB | | 768×768 | **1.7** | **2118** | 13.7 GB | | 1024×1024 | OOM on allocating 4.5 GB | — | — | Gotchas that prevent anything from running: cuDNN 9 on sm_70 can't find an engine for convolutions (`GET was unable to find an engine to execute this computation`) - both on HF SDXL repo and local checkpoints. Fixed by `torch.backends.cudnn.enabled = False`; afterwards, 512 and 768 work fine, but 1024 falls out of memory because without cuDNN convolutions are computed via im2col and require four times more VRAM. VAE in fp32 doesn't work - latent fp16 results in `Input type (c10::Half) and bias type (float)`; VAE remains fp16, slicing and tiling are enabled. Practical conclusion: for commercial cards, 768 px is the working mode (35 cards per minute), while 1024 px and printing require either fixing cuDNN for Volta or upscaling from 768 (V100-06). **Could not download the official checkpoint - HF limits the output.** Attempts: full load via `curl` gets stuck at 8-16 KB, range download goes up to 3-4 GB and fails, once reached 18.24 GB but with extra 7 KB at the end and failed to load (`failed to load model` even after trimming to exact size). Around 60 GB downloaded from this IP in a day, then server started giving 8 KB per request. Background repeats left running (`/srv/media/night/glm_loop.log`, up to 60 cycles of 5 minutes each). Once the file arrives intact, the harness run will take 20 minutes, weights are already prepared.
