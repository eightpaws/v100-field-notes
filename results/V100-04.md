# V100-04 — VLM as a card reject controller

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** Before publishing a card, it's cheaper to run the image through a VLM-judge in the category (artefacts, distorted text on packaging, extraneous elements, "blurry" color) than to catch these in a batch of 200.

**Environment.** V100-SXM2-16GB (sm_70), driver 580.178.04; venv `/home/hermes/venvs/cu121` (transformers, torch cu121), model `Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct` in fp16, `HF_HOME=/srv/media/ai-cache/hf`.

**Commands.** `python3 /srv/media/projects/qa-vlm/qa_vlm_single.py` (free response for 24 images) and `python3 /srv/media/projects/qa-vlm/qa_vlm_strict.py` (strict prompt "ok/brak" for 10 images, annotation in constant `CHECK`).

**Artifacts.** srv/media/projects/qa-vlm/vlm_single.json, vlm_strict.json, meta.json; logs /srv/media/night/qa_vlm.log, qa_vlm_single.log, qa_vlm_strict.log.

**Repeats.** Single run per prompt (same images, temperature 1e-06); no dispersion was removed.

**Result.** Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct (fp16, `device_map=cuda`) on 24 generated cards 640x640 produced **24 out of 24 "good"**, **1.8 sec per image** (45.4 sec per batch), peak VRAM **7,634 MiB**. Strict prompt "one word" on 10 images, with 1/3/5 marked as good and 2/7/12 as defective, responded `ok` to all ten - **did not catch any of the three marked defects**. Verdict: As an automatic controller before publication, 3B-model is not suitable, it approves everything; for rejection, either 7B with category and threshold, or human review is needed.
