# V100-101 — LoRA under the product and brand

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** In the workflow, slots for LoRA are provided, meaning the style and recognizability are maintained by weights, not by the prompt. For a store, this is a way to obtain consistent output without long prompts and without drift between batches.

**Environment.** Tesla V100 16 GB (sm_70), driver 580, torch 2.10.0+cu128, diffusers 0.39.0, transformers 5.24.0, peft 0.22.0, Python 3.12, cuDNN disabled; weights RealVisXL_V5.0_fp16.safetensors

**Commands.** ``` /root/bin/v100-run.sh V100-101 -- env STEPS=2400 /var/lib/ollama/venvs/video/bin/python /root/v101/train_lora.py; evaluation: env STEPS_LIST=1600,2400 .../eval_steps.py; dataset: .../make_dataset.py (25 frames 640x640, 28 steps, cfg 5.0). ```

**Artifacts.** Frames /srv/media/v101/eval/ (base, steps 200/400/800/1600/2400, sheets sheet3_a/b.jpg), weights /srv/media/v101/lora/step-*, logs /srv/media/v101/logs/train-2400.log and eval-steps-200-400.log. /root/runs/V100-101/run.log is overwritten by the next run of the same ID.

**Repeats.** Training: Single run; Evaluation: Pairwise on 10 prompts with fixed seeds, n=10.

**Result.** LoRA on 24 frames of a single product (640 px, rank 8, lr 1e-4): 800 steps in 16.1 min, 2400 steps in 48.9 min, peak VRAM 7.8 GiB. Similarity to reference frame (CLIP ViT-L/14, cosine): base SDXL 0.6750 · 200 steps 0.6756 · 400 0.6804 · 800 0.6898 · **1600 0.6943** · 2400 0.6788, indicating a plateau around 1600 steps and regression at 2400. Recognizable packaging (shape, matte sage tin, brass lid, cream rectangular label) in 4-5 out of 10 frames compared to 2-3 in the base. "8 out of 10" criterion not met.


**Verdict.** LoRA on 24 frames of a single product (640 px, rank 8, lr 1e-4): 800 steps in 16.1 min, 2400 steps in 48.9 min, peak VRAM 7.8 GiB.
