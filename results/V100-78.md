# V100-78 - Two fast mid-size models through one harness

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) in a VM: driver 580.178.04, CUDA 12.9, 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM._

**Question.** Shop copy (product cards, letters to clients) needs answers that arrive instantly, hold a
format, come in the asked volume, stay inside a supplied fact block and admit ignorance. Which of the
two candidates does that on this card: Nemotron-Bolt-E4B or SenseNova-Loop-9B?

**Environment.** V100-SXM2-16GB (sm_70), llama.cpp build129 for the first model and `ik_llama.cpp`
(build `ac21694`) for the second, because upstream llama.cpp rejects `sensenova_loop` as an unknown
architecture. Files: `NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-UD-Q4_K_XL.gguf` (5 060 482 240 bytes, arch gemma4,
7.52 B params) and `SenseNova-Loop-9B-Q4_K_M.gguf` (5 818 631 072 bytes, 8.96 B params).

**Commands.**
```
llama-bench -m <file> -p 512 -n 64 -r 3
llama-server -m <file> -ngl 99 -c 8192 --host 127.0.0.1 --port 18495 -t 8 --jinja
python3 /root/bin/mid_eval.py --base-url http://127.0.0.1:18495 --label <name> --out <json>
python3 /root/bin/honesty_recheck.py --base-url ... --label <name> --out <json>
```
The harness runs five strict-format tasks (JSON card, letter with a subject line, exactly five numbered
items, a markdown table with fixed columns, a one-word answer), two volume tasks (400-character product
description, 120-word letter), three questions answerable only from a supplied fact block, and three
trap questions about things that do not exist.

**Repeats.** Decode: three generations of 64 tokens per model; every quality block is a single run.

**Result.**

| Model | decode, tok/s | format | volume | RAG | honesty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nemotron-Bolt-E4B | **109.56 +/- 0.29** (bench), 95.2-96.4 through the server | **5/5** | **2/2** | **3/3** | **3/3** |
| SenseNova-Loop-9B | 56.20 (bench), 48.5-49.5 through the server | 5/5 | 2/2 | 3/3 | 3/3 |

Nemotron holds 4 011 MiB and reaches 7274 tok/s of prefill at pp512; SenseNova needs 468.7 tok/s at
pp128 and only runs on the fork. Both models keep the format, produce the asked volume, answer strictly
from the fact block and hedge on all three traps.

Correction worth recording: the first honesty pass scored 2/3 and 0/3, but the classifier was missing
phrases the models actually used ("no information", "outside my knowledge", "cannot say" - translated from the Russian wording the models used). Re-run
with a wider list, both models hedged on all three traps - the low score was a harness bug, not a model
failure.

**Verdict.** Positive: **Nemotron-Bolt-E4B is the working model for shop text** - faster than the 90 tok/s
bar by a comfortable margin, 4 GB of VRAM, clean on all four quality blocks. SenseNova is its equal on
quality but at half the speed and behind a fork, so it stays a spare.

**Result type.** Unstated - the result reports measurements without an explicit pass/fail verdict.

**Does not show.** Neither model was tested on real catalogue data: the fact block is synthetic and the
traps are invented. Long letters above 800 tokens, multi-turn dialogue and tool calling were not
exercised. Russian fluency was judged only by whether the format held, not by style.

**Raw.** `raw/runs/V100-78.json` - both benches, both server runs, per-task detail, and the honesty
re-run note.
