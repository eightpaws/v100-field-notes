# V100-24 — DRY-sampler and budget of reasoning

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 96 GB RAM, driver 580._

**Hypothesis.** On V100 16 GB (issue #28932) the DRY-penalty reduced the repeat share from 0.271 to 0.085, and the worst cyclic grammar from 41 to 2; with `--reasoning-budget 4096` - down to 0.039. This is not speed, but the elimination of the most annoying defect of local models.

**Test rig.** Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB (cc 7.0, 16384 MiB) · driver 580.178.04 · Ubuntu 24.04.5, kernel 6.8.0-151 · EPYC 7282, 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM · Python 3.12.3 · ollama: KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0, NUM_PARALLEL=4, KEEP_ALIVE=30m, MAX_LOADED_MODELS=1

**Commands.** `--dry-penalty-last-n 1024 --dry-multiplier 0.8 --dry-base 1.75`; 5 regular tasks + 2 specifically cycling (120 types of roses by numbers, 80 lines of log), modes: base / DRY / DRY+budget.

**Numbers.**

| mode | repeated 12-gram share | tok/s |
|---|---|---|
| baseline | 0.0 | 67.14 |
| DRY | 0.0 | 65.20 |
| DRY + reasoning budget | 0.0 | 65.30 |

**Artifacts.** [raw/runs/V100-24.json](../raw/runs/V100-24.json)

**Repeats.** 3 modes × 7 prompts, n=1 per mode

**What is needed.** llama.cpp with `--dry-penalty-last-n 1024 --dry-multiplier 0.8 --dry-base 1.75` and `--reasoning-budget 4096`; the same 5 prompts as in our code and lists tests.

**How it was verified.** Share of 12-gram repeats and worst repeat before/after; at temp=0 the response should match, except where the cycle previously started.

**Risks.** Parameters are tuned for a specific model and may "dry" the style; DRY is off by default (`--dry-penalty-last-n 0`) - it's easy to forget to turn it on.

**Result.** The DRY penalty did not reproduce on our model: on five regular tasks and two specifically designed to cycle (120 varieties of roses by numbers, 80 lines of log) the share of repeated 12-grams was 0.0 in all modes - both without DRY and with `--dry-penalty-last-n 1024 --dry-multiplier 0.8 --dry-base 1.75`. Cycling does not occur at all in qwen2.5-coder-14b with temp=0 on Russian tasks, there is nothing to treat. The cost of the mode is approximately 3% speed (67.1 vs 65.2 tok/s). Conclusion: keep DRY turned off, turn it on selectively if a specific model starts cycling; `--reasoning-budget` build accepts.
