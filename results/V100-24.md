# V100-24 — DRY-sampler and budget of reasoning

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 96 GB RAM, driver 580._

**Hypothesis.** On V100 16 GB (issue #28932) the DRY-penalty reduced the repeat share from 0.271 to 0.085, and the worst cyclic grammar from 41 to 2; with `--reasoning-budget 4096` - down to 0.039. This is not speed, but the elimination of the most annoying defect of local models.

**What is needed.** llama.cpp with `--dry-penalty-last-n 1024 --dry-multiplier 0.8 --dry-base 1.75` and `--reasoning-budget 4096`; the same 5 prompts as in our code and lists tests.

**How it was verified.** Share of 12-gram repeats and worst repeat before/after; at temp=0 the response should match, except where the cycle previously started.

**Risks.** Parameters are selected for a specific model and may "dry" the style; DRY is disabled by default (`--dry-penalty-last-n 0`) - it's easy to forget to enable it.

**Result.** The DRY penalty did not reproduce on our model: on five regular tasks and two specifically cycling (120 varieties of roses by numbers, 80 lines of log) the share of repeated 12-grams was 0.0 in all modes - both without DRY and with `--dry-penalty-last-n 1024 --dry-multiplier 0.8 --dry-base 1.75`. Cycling does not occur at all in qwen2.5-coder-14b with temp=0 on Russian tasks, there is nothing to treat. The cost of the mode is about 3% speed (67.1 against 65.2 tok/s). Conclusion: keep DRY turned off, turn it on selectively if a specific model starts cycling; the `--reasoning-budget` build accepts.
