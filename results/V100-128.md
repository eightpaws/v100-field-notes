_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Environment.** llama.cpp 1.0.0-dev (build 1, commit b1b770d, build CUDA 12.9, sm_70), driver 580.178.04, Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct Q8_0 (8.1 GB), Python 3.12.3, pytest 9.2.0, coverage 7.17.5, guest ai-gpu (V100 16 GB).

**Repeats.** 3 generations of 18 tests each: passed on the standard 4/3/4, coverage 22.8/22.2/22.8%, caught 1/10 in all three. VRAM peak 8281 MiB, generation 23.4s including model loading.

**Result.** No: three runs, all sets red on correct code (14 out of 18 tests fail). Coverage by passing tests is 22.2-22.8%, catches 1 out of 10 planted defects (rounding substitution). Criterion (60% coverage and half of defects) not met. Defects are similar: 9 out of 14 - NameError on BillingError/CouponError (model writes pytest.raises but does not import exceptions), others - incorrect expectations (progressive tariffs, tax rate in percentage instead of fraction, rounding proportion). Claimed 83.6% coverage and 10/10 mutations first count - artifact of red set: failing test fails on any mutation; recalculation by tests green on standard gives 1/10.
