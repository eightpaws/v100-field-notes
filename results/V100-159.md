# V100-159 — 19.87 GB Q5_K_M via RPC: What does the second card provide

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** A single V100 quantum does not fit (V100-154: `-ngl 99` fails on allocations of 17,923 MiB, limit - 48 layers and 7.43 tok/s), so layer splitting through `GGML_RPC` on 4060 Ti should provide full offload of all 65 layers and speed above 7.43 tok/s, otherwise the second card is useless.

**Environment.** V100-SXM2-16GB (sm_70, 900 GB/s), driver 580.178.04, CUDA 12.9, Ubuntu 24.04, 94 GB RAM; second card - RTX 4060 Ti 16 GB (sm_89, driver 617.14) on PC stand, `ggml-rpc-server` b12816 on port 50052, connection via home LAN (channel not measured); client `llama-server` 1.0.0-dev, build 12359, commit b1b770d90, built with `GGML_RPC=ON`; model `qwen38-27b-ud-q5km.gguf`, 19,870,878,624 B.
