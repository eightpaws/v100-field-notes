# V100-95 — The 4060 Ti can: 3D modeling, ray tracing, video editing, machine learning inference.
The V100 cannot: real-time ray tracing, video encoding.

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** sm_89 provides bf16, FP8, Marlin INT4 and native flash-attention; on V100 all this is either unavailable or slow (AWQ on sm_70 loses quality - V100-17, cuDNN on our build does not work - V100-54). Therefore, some tasks are more advantageous to offload to PC not by speed, but by capabilities.

**Environment.** PC: RTX 4060 Ti 16GB, cc 8.9, driver 617.14, Windows 11, Ollama 0.40.17, Model entirely in VRAM 4.43 GB. Guest ai-gpu: Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB, cc 7.0, driver 580.178.04, Ubuntu 24.04, Ollama 0.40.16 (library cuda_v12, log raises CUDA0 at cc 7.0), Model in VRAM 4.58 GB.

**Commands.** `python3 b95_speed.py http://127.0.0.1:11434 qwen2.5-coder:7b <out> 3 256` and `python3 b95_ctx.py http://127.0.0.1:11434 qwen2.5-coder:7b <out> 2 300 64` (num_ctx 16384), quality - `python3 b95_worker.py --tasks v100_97_tasks.json --ids 0-19 --base http://127.0.0.1:11434 --model qwen2.5-coder:7b --out <out>`; on V100 run via `/root/bin/v100-run.sh V100-95 -- <same line>`.

**Artifacts.** /root/runs/V100-95/run.log; JSON for V100 - /srv/media/night/v100_95_speed_v100.json, v100_95_mbpp_v100.json, v100_95_ctx_v100.json; on PC the same three files with suffix _pc.

**Repeats.** Speed - n=3, mean ± std dev (decode V100 103.1 ± 0.0; PC 54.0 ± 0.25); Quality - single run, 20 tasks, seed fixed.

**Result.**

**Verdict.** The hypothesis was partially confirmed. On the same scales (qwen2.5-coder:7b Q4_K_M, digest dae161e27b0e on both machines), V100 decodes 103.1 ± 0.0 tok/s, 4060 Ti - 54.0 ± 0.25 tok/s, making the PC 1.9 times slower: 900 GB/s vs 288 GB/s.
