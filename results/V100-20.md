Rejected along with V100-22: this is the same fni8 package family (int8 dp4a for DiT in ComfyUI). The author himself writes that acceleration was measured on a CMP 100-210 park with reduced fp16 cores and is not reproduced on a real Tesla V100 - our fp16 tensor cores are healthy (46 TOP/s by our measurement of V100-19), so the int8 path through CUDA cores will be slower. Additional argument against: the package requires converting models to the `.fni8` format (custom weight file), meaning a modification of our SDXL/FLUX pipeline. Quality is claimed at PSNR 29-31 dB with SSIM 0.95 - for commercial photos, this is a noticeable loss, and there's no gain on our silicon. Our working path for images remains: diffusers + torch 2.10.0+cu128, VAE in fp16 without upcast, 13.3 s per frame at 1216x832.
