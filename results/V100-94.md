# V100-94 — Images on 4060 Ti, code on V100

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** The image factory held a V100 for 6 hours and consumed five ticks of the queue in a row. On the 4060 Ti (Ada, 16 GB, SDXL/turbo), generation should proceed comparably fast, while the V100 remains under code. This is not model acceleration, but idle elimination - the cheapest win of all.

**Environment.** RTX 4060 Ti 16 GB (sm_89, 16 380 MiB), driver 617.14, Windows 11; torch 2.11.0+cu128, diffusers 0.43.0, cuDNN 91900 (works on Ada, not on sm_70); `StableDiffusionXLPipeline.from_single_file` fp16, VAE `force_upcast=False`, slicing and tiling; checkpoint `DreamShaperXL_Turbo_v2_1.safetensors` (6 939 220 250 B) - same as baseline V100-73; guidance 0.0.

**Commands.** `/d/venvs/image/Scripts/python.exe D:/Programs/hermes/cache/scratch/v94_pc.py` - 12 prompts per mode, warm-up 512 px / 2 steps before measurements, then three consecutive repeats for each mode.

**Artifacts.** srv/media/night/v100_94_pc.json (line for mode and repeat), full log of my run D:/Programs/hermes/cache/scratch/v94_run.log, 108 frames in D:/Programs/hermes/cache/scratch/v94/.
