# V100-05 — IC-Light relighting: the product colour moves by 24-37 dE

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** Instead of compositing a product into a new scene with ControlNet, relight the already-photographed product to match a scene. For a flower shop this would turn white-background catalogue shots into in-context images. Success criterion: colour shift below dE 3 at three points of the product, at most 10 s per photo.

**Environment.** Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB, cc 7.0, driver 580, CUDA 12.9 toolkit; IC-Light v2 (`iclight_sd15_fc.safetensors`, 1.72 GB) on the SD1.5 base kept in `/srv/media/projects/iclight/rv51`; diffusers 0.39.0, torch 2.10.0+cu128, Python 3.12; frame 512x512, 30 steps, guidance 2.0, foreground mask from `rmbg`.

**Commands.** The exact launch line was not preserved. Parameters come from the driver scripts `/srv/media/projects/iclight/iclight_run.py` (three text scenes) and `iclight_neutral.py` (neutral light): `W, H = 512, 512`, `STEPS = 30`, `guidance_scale=2.0`, source frames in `/srv/media/generated/images/v100_2411_1208`.

**Artifacts.** [`raw/runs/V100-05.json`](../raw/runs/V100-05.json) - per-pair dE at three points of the product for all three scenes and for the neutral pass.

**Repeats.** Single run, one pass per frame; no repeats were made, so no spread is quoted.

**Result.**

| Metric | Value | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| pairs with a coloured scene | 30 | pairs |
| dE mean, coloured scenes | 37.5 | dE76 |
| dE max, coloured scenes | 65.9 | dE76 |
| dE mean, neutral light | 24.1 | dE76 |
| dE best frame, neutral light | 8.4 | dE76 |
| acceptance threshold from the card | 3.0 | dE76 |
| time per photo | 1.9-2.0 | s |
| VRAM peak | 7132 | MiB |

Ten products (houseplants, a dried bouquet, an orchid), each relit under warm interior, cold studio and sunny morning light, plus one neutral-light pass. Not a single frame stays inside the dE 3 threshold.

**Verdict.** Not usable for a product card: the product itself changes colour 8 to 22 times beyond tolerance under any light, so the only honest use is repainting the scene behind a masked, untouched product.

**Result type.** Negative.

**Does not show.** No human visual acceptance was done, only dE at three points; the 512 px output was never checked at print resolution; the reference-image mode of IC-Light was not measured, only text-described scenes; the contribution of the `rmbg` mask to the object boundary was not evaluated.
