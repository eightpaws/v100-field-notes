_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** The V100-131 compared coding of 7B Q4_K_M and 27B IQ3_S, where size and quantization changed along with denoising, and "coding loss" was not measured.
