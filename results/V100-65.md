# V100-65 — Recheck 8k and 16k and set num\_batch to 4096

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** On 8k Optimum may shift to 1024-2048, and 4096 is capable of causing a drop or OOM because the batch occupies VRAM along with KV.

**Environment.** llama.cpp, `llama-bench` pp2048/8192/16384, r=1; V100 16 GB (sm_70), driver 580, CUDA 12.9
