# V100-16 — Volta-fa patches for llama.cpp

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 96 GB RAM, driver 580._

**Hypothesis.** Frostbite1536/volta-fa - eight small patches on a fixed commit of llama.cpp: crash of FA tests on sm_70, GQA-attention on small batches, quantized KV cache, prefill at D=256, new tensor matmul for K-quantization. Claimed +34–45 % improvement where we live: one user, 8–16 tokens in a batch, long context.

**Test rig.** Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB (cc 7.0, 16384 MiB) · driver 580.178.04 · Ubuntu 24.04.5, kernel 6.8.0-151 · EPYC 7282, 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM · Python 3.12.3 · ollama: KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0, NUM_PARALLEL=4, KEEP_ALIVE=30m, MAX_LOADED_MODELS=1

**Commands.** Build: `cmake -DGGML_CUDA=ON -DCMAKE_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES=70`, then `llama-bench -p 512 -n 128` against Ollama on the same model.

**Numbers.**

| build | decode tok/s | prefill 512 | prefill 4096 | patches applied |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ollama (upstream) | 65.8 | 1776 | — | 0 |
| llama.cpp + FA | 70.65 | 1822 | 1663 | 4 of 8 |

**Artifacts.** binary not saved (build was in /tmp), patches — Frostbite1536/volta-fa

**Repeats.** single run, n=1

**What is needed.** llama.cpp on commit 79e2e74, `git am` applied to eight patches, built with `-DGGML_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES=70` on CUDA 12.8; our GGUF Q4_K are already available; qwen2.5-coder-14b as the main test.

**How it was verified.** Repeat their harness on our map: decode tg64 and prefill at 1k/8k/32k, separately quantized KV q8_0/q4_0. Comparison with the current build of llama.cpp/Ollama on the same prompts. Criterion: ≥20% on decode or ≥30% on prefill, quality bit-for-bit at temp=0.

**Risks.** Patches are not rebased on new commits - fixing the build; CUDA 12.9 has a weight allocation bug on sm_70 (llama.cpp #28416); with 16 GB, part of their tests (27B) is unavailable - only 7–14B.

**Result.** Built llama.cpp with 4 of 8 patches (the rest did not apply - base-commit in the patches was not specified). Compared to Ollama: decode 70.65 against 65.8 tok/s (+7.4%), prefill 512 - 1822 against 1776 (+2.6%), prefill 4096 - 1663. The advertised +34–45% is not there; almost all the gain comes from FlashAttention itself (+8.7% to decode), not the patches. Conclusion: it's not worth leaving Ollama for a 7% improvement. Log: 22.11.2026.
