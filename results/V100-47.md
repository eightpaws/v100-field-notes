# V100-47 — DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite 16B MoE

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** A 16B MoE with 2.4B active weights in Q4 weighs approximately 8.9 GB: it fits entirely, leaving space for 32k context, while the speed should be closer to that of a 7B model (30-40 tok/s) with quality above 14B - the model was trained on code and retains 128k in the original version.

**Result.** Upstream llama.cpp b7743fbfd8 (build V100-26, CUDA 12.0), DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite-Instruct Q4_K_M 9,65 GB, V100 16 GB, -ngl 99: without FA prefill pp256 2250 ± 139 tok/s, decode tg64 **183.6 ± 0.5 tok/s**; with `-fa 1` prefill 228.8, decode 1.14 tok/s, that is, FA drops the speed by 160 times on this model. The best decode from all measured on the card: MoE 15.7 billion with 2.4 billion active. Operating mode: `-ngl 99 -fa 0`. Generation quality and tool calling have not yet been verified (a fresh build of llama.cpp is needed, the old one does not know about arch deepseek2 in FA mode and does not load Qwen3-Coder).
