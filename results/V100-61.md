# V100-61 - GQA to MHA: no pipeline in this stack carries the change

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) in a VM: driver 580.178.04, CUDA 12.9, 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM._

**Question.** V100-19 measured a 7.2x gain from expanding grouped-query attention to multi-head at the
kernel level on one shape. Does that survive end to end - frame time in an image pipeline or response time
in a chat model - or does attention turn out to be too small a share of the work?

**Environment.** The card expects a GQA model from our own pipelines or Qwen-VL. Inventory of what is
actually on this machine:

| Carrier | Attention | Can the K/V be expanded? |
|---|---|---|
| SDXL, Flux.1-schnell, SD3.5-large | MHA | nothing to expand |
| Wan2.2, LTX-video | MHA | nothing to expand |
| Qwen3.8 27B under llama.cpp | GQA | no switch exists |
| Qwen-VL | absent | - |

**Commands.** None were run: the inventory above is the whole experiment. Checking the pipelines means
reading their attention configuration, and checking llama.cpp means its flags - neither exposes a GQA-to-MHA
toggle, so there is no configuration to A/B.

**Repeats.** Not applicable.

**Result.** The card cannot be answered in this stack as it stands. Every diffusion pipeline we run is plain
multi-head attention, so there is no grouped-query K/V to expand; the one GQA family we serve (Qwen3.8) runs
under llama.cpp, which has no such switch; and the extension from V100-19 is a standalone CUDA kernel that
neither llama.cpp nor diffusers calls.

**Verdict.** Unstated, and deliberately not a "no effect" claim: the honest statement is that the test has
no carrier, not that the effect is absent. Measuring it means building a pipeline around the V100-19
extension - wiring its K/V expansion into a real model, then timing frames with it on and off. That is a
separate job of roughly four to six hours, and it belongs to whoever picks up the parent card again.

**Result type.** Unstated - the result reports measurements without an explicit pass/fail verdict.

**Does not show.** Nothing about the end-to-end effect - neither its size nor its absence. The kernel-level
7.2x from V100-19 is untouched by this note and still stands for the single shape it was measured on.

**Raw.** `raw/runs/V100-61.json` - the carrier inventory and the reason the criterion is unreachable.
