# V100-10 — Prefill and batching: where V100 actually loses out

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 96 GB RAM, driver 580._

**Hypothesis.** In the code tests, V100 prefill is 360–703 tok/s, while in the comparison of the same models on "Two Machines" it's 1708 against 1726 on the PC, that is, parity. This means that the variance is due to defaults and methodology (ubatch, num_ctx, absence of FA), not hardware. Plus, a separate hypothesis: at a 32k+ context, the gap will appear because the PC has FA2, while V100 has xformers/math.

**What is needed.** llama.cpp with explicit `-b/-ub` and Ollama with num_batch; the same 14B Q4; prompts of 1k / 8k / 32k tokens; nvidia-smi throughout.

**How it was verified.** prefill tok/s at ub 128 / 512 / 1024 / 2048 and contexts 1k / 8k / 32k, VRAM peak. Find the point where V100 starts losing to PC and by how many times. Criterion: on 1k and 8k the gap ≤15 %.

**Risks.** Ollama does not expose all the handles - a llama.cpp server is needed; 32k without q8 KV won't fit (dependency on V100-02).

**Result.** prefill 1776 tok/s at `num_batch=2048` against 587 at 128 and 1351 at default 512 - ×3 to 128 and +31 % to 512, on 1k, 8k and 16k the best is exactly 2048. On 16 440 tokens 1441 tok/s (-19 % to short). Conclusion: on V100 keep `num_batch=2048`. Journal: 22.11.2026.
