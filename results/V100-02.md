# V100-02 — KV cache q8_0 → context 32–64k

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 96 GB RAM, driver 580._

**Hypothesis.** KV in fp16 for 14B - approximately 2 GB for an 8k context. Quantized q8 cuts this in half, meaning 32k fits without offloading to RAM, and on Volta, the risk of fp16 overflow in the cache is also eliminated.

**What is needed.** llama.cpp or Ollama with `num_ctx` and q8 KV (`-ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0`); a long real document of 30–50k tokens (contract, catalog, log).

**How it was verified.** 32k and 64k on 7B and 14B: fit/don't fit, VRAM peak, prefill speed, and needle test - question from the middle of the document. Criterion: 32k on 14B without offload and the needle response is correct.

**Risks.** Without FA the prefill speed will drop; q8 KV on long chains sometimes loses accuracy - measure it specifically on your documents.

**Result.** q8_0 KV search does not damage (needle 3/3 on 6496 tokens, as with fp16) and at 16k provides twice the generation speed - 44 against 21 tok/s, plus 2 GB of free VRAM (11.6 against 13.6). But the ceiling for 14B on 16 GB is 32k context (16k prompt): 64k does not load even with q8. Part of the hypothesis about 64k is rejected. Journal: 22.11.2026.
