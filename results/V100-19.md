# V100-19 — GQA→MHA: quiet fallback attention in ComfyUI

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 96 GB RAM, driver 580._

**Hypothesis.** On V100 SDPA with GQA cannot use fused cutlass and falls back to math-backend without error: 4.30 against 34.36 TFLOP/s on the same shape. Fixed by expanding K/V projections to MHA (ComfyUI-RelayVSR-V100 node does this for 1.9x on decoder). Our LTX/Wan/Flux are exactly the candidates.

**What is needed.** ComfyUI (or diffusers if our pipeline), check `torch.backends.cuda.can_use_efficient_attention()` and log of the selected backend, test on LTX-Video and SDXL/Flux.

**How it was verified.** First, measure which backend is actually used for each model (math vs efficient vs FA); then repeat the form with expanded K/V and compare seconds and VRAM. Criterion: on video pipeline ≥1.5×.

**Risks.** MHA-extension copies weights (+78 MB, +39 ms on initialization) - this is noticeable for 16 GB; for models with a large number of KV heads, the memory gain may turn into a memory loss.

**Result.** Confirmed: GQA silently falls back to the math path on sm_70: 4.63 TF/s compared to 33.15 for MHA (×7.2), on 8192 math tokens it consumes 8 GB and crashes due to OOM. Expanding KV to MHA returns 31.9 TF/s. Our LTX/Wan/CogVideoX/SDXL are MHA, they are not affected; the rule is needed for GQA models (Qwen-VL, new video-DiT). Check: compare auto with forced MATH - if it matches, math has been enabled.
