# V100-83 — Strata-V100: third engine for verification

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** `jmnargi/Strata-V100` — community-supported fork of the Strata engine specifically for Volta, with its own benchmark table on V100 and expert layout by quants. Provides an independent reference point for 1Cat-vLLM (V100-58) and llama.cpp.

**Environment.** Strata 0.1.45.13, engine `engine-cuda12`, CUDA 12.8, driver 580.178.04, V100-SXM2-16GB, weights on `/srv/ssd-strata` (ssd-pool via NFS).

**Repeats.** n=2 to the shoulder, spread 0.4-0.8 tok/s. **Why not reached claimed.** Three different reasons, can't blame them all on one: 1. Speed 131 tok/s - that's two cards with layer-by-layer split; on one card, the documentation itself gives 28-33 tok/s, and we are within this corridor, not below. 2. Context 262k - on a 32-gigabyte card; we have 16 GB, and in the config it is set to `--max-context 32768`. Long context is limited by VRAM, not the engine. 3. Quality (SWE-bench Pro 62.5, LiveCodeBench 91.9) relates to the full model in bf16/fp8. We are running IQ1_M (58.4 GB, around 1.6 bits per weight) and UD-IQ4_XS (93.7 GB, 2 bits), and on them MBPP 0.53-0.56 - the same level as 30B-A3B in Q4. This is the price of a quantum, not the engine.
