# V100-132 — Captcha: Local VLM vs Service

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** Paid CAPTCHA solvers cost $0.80-3 per 1000 ($0.80 for reCAPTCHA v2 at CapSolver, $1.45 for Turnstile at 2captcha) and operate on humans and ML. Fact needed: Can a V100 handle visual CAPTCHAs and at what accuracy is its own hardware cheaper? Application - **only own resources and stands** (testing own protection, form tests, access to own accounts); other sites are against their rules and not considered.
