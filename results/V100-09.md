# V100-09 — 360° Product: Photogrammetry or Splines

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** 20-40 product photos in a circle → 3D model or Gaussian splines → short rotation video. Most experimental direction: provides what no other competitor in the niche has and runs entirely on its own hardware.

**Environment.** Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB (sm_70), driver 580, torch 2.10.0+cu128, gsplat 1.5.3 (JIT build: nvcc 12.9, gcc-12, `TORCH_CUDA_ARCH_LIST=7.0`, `MAX_JOBS=8`), pycolmap 4.2.1 (wheel without CUDA), Ubuntu 24.04, EPYC 7282, 94 GB RAM.
