# V100-30 — sm70-attn: FA2-D256 in llama.cpp

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** Deep fork of llama.cpp: ported Split-D D256 kernel from 1CatAI, SplitKV3 (three-way KV split for long prefix), fully f32-output attention, direct q4_0 read into MMA fragments. Claimed: prefill 174k +36.2 %, decode 46.9 tok/s, TP2 over NVLink.

**Environment.** Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB (sm_70), driver 580, CUDA 12.9, guest VM on a home Proxmox host; engine: not recorded in the artifacts; model: not recorded in the artifacts.

**Commands.** Not run: the card was rejected after reading the sources, no experiment took place.

**Artifacts.** None, and none can exist: the card was rejected before any run.

**Repeats.** Not recorded: the field became mandatory only after this run, so repeatability is not documented - treat the numbers as a single run unless the result says otherwise.

**Result.** Rejected based on the card criterion: "do we have a model with head_dim 256 and do we need context over 64k". The answer is no and no. What was actually checked: - We don't have models with head_dim 256 in llama.cpp format. The 27B-class exists only as a PXQN file for the PXA engine (V100-29), its cores cannot be connected to llama.cpp. - Context over 64k is not needed for our tasks: we handle code up to 8-32k (Qwen3-Coder reaches 25 tok/s at 32k on V100-51), and we don't process long documents. - Practical testing of long context (V100-18) showed that even with compressed KV prefill, processing 64k takes a minute for 12 thousand tokens, while the model was trained on 32k. Thus, the bottleneck is not the form of attention kernels but the volume of prefill. Optimization is specific: Split-D D256 and SplitKV3 provide gains for 27B with 16 layers of full attention at 64k+ context. For our models (head_dim 128, context up to 32k), there will be no gain, and the fork would have to be maintained under a specific commit and updated manually. It makes sense to revisit the card if a GGUF model of 27B-class with head_dim 256 appears and a task requiring context over 64k arises.
