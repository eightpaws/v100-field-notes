# V100-40 — ik_llama.cpp vs upstream llama.cpp on V100

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** The ik_llama.cpp fork advertises roughly 2.2x on V100 (prefill 2319 vs 1001, decode 74.7 vs 33.6 tok/s) on a MoE model with flash attention. Does that reproduce on a 16 GB SXM2 card with our own toolchain?

**Environment.**

| Component | Version |
|---|---|
| GPU / driver / compute cap | Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB / 580.178.04 / 7.0 |
| Engines | ik_llama.cpp ac21694 vs upstream llama.cpp master 51831d4, both built from source |
| Runtime | CUDA 12.9.41, GCC 13 host compiler, `-DCMAKE_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES=70` |
| Host | Proxmox VM, 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, Ubuntu 24.04 |

**Commands.**

```bash
# ik_llama.cpp (needs CUDA_HOME with headers and nvcc 12.9; CUDA 12.0 cannot compile its f8 kernels)
cmake -S . -B build129 -DGGML_CUDA=ON -DCMAKE_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES=70 -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release \
      -DLLAMA_CURL=OFF -DLLAMA_BUILD_HTML=OFF -DCMAKE_CUDA_HOST_COMPILER=/usr/bin/g++-12
cmake --build build129 -j 12 --target llama-bench

llama-bench -m qwen2.5-coder-14b.gguf -p 512 -n 128 -ngl 99 -fa 0|1 -r 2
```

**Results.**

Qwen2.5-Coder-14B Q4_K_M, `-ngl 99`, median of two runs:

| Engine | FA | prefill pp512 tok/s | decode tg128 tok/s |
|---|---|---|---|
| ik_llama | off | 890 | 69.06 |
| ik_llama | on | 843 | **73.53** |
| upstream | off | 1638 | 65.39 |
| upstream | on | **1779** | 70.92 |

**Repeats.** n=2 per configuration, mean reported by llama-bench (stdev ≤ 1% on decode, up to 16% on prefill).

**Verdict.** The advertised 2.2x does not reproduce: prefill on the fork is about half of upstream (843–890 vs 1638–1779 tok/s) and decode is only 4% better with flash attention (73.53 vs 70.92). On DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite (arch deepseek2) the fork crashes with a core dump at startup, while upstream reaches 2250 tok/s prefill and 183.6 tok/s decode. Stay on upstream.

**Result type.** Negative.

**Does not show.** The fork's own numbers were measured on 4x V100 32 GB with NVLink and a different model, so this is a single-card, single-model comparison; large-batch throughput and multi-GPU paths were not tested.
