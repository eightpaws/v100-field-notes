_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** Setting up from a snapshot without network will bring up all three pipelines; if not, the snapshot is missing packages, which should be known before updating, not after.

**Environment.** Guest AI-GPU on home bench, Ubuntu 24.04, kernel 6.8.0-151-generic, Python 3.12.3, driver 580.178.04, nvcc release 12.9 (`/home/hermes/cuda-12.9`), pip from fresh venv; network removed `unshare -n` (only loopback remains, DNS not responding).

 No: snapshot does not raise stack without network. In cache, 12 wheels for 2.3 GB cover 8 lines out of 302 in full freeze (2.6%) and 15 out of 45 in `acestep/requirements.txt` (33%). Installing full freeze fails on the third line (`-e git+https://github.com/ace-step/...`, git requires network), and positive control `torch==2.10.0+cu128` fails on `filelock`: there are no transitive dependencies in cache, including 42 packages `nvidia-*` from freeze. Should have removed 23 GB of wheels (that's how much three live venv take: 8.4 + 14 + 1.0 GB), not 2.3 GB. Run took 15 s, card was idle (0 MiB), 63 GB left on root out of 193.
