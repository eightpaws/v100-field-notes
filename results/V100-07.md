# V100-07 - Chatterbox Multilingual on a V100: expressiveness for free, 40-second ceiling

_Measured on Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70), driver 580.178.04, inside a Proxmox guest on the home rig._

**Question.** Silero and F5-TTS read advertisement copy flat: no emphasis, no pauses, no dialogue. VibeVoice-1.5B and Chatterbox Multilingual claim emotion and multi-speaker output and are free. Does the Russian voice of Chatterbox on a 16 GB V100 hold intelligibility for a 60-second ad read, how fast is it, and does it survive long forms.

**Environment.**
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| GPU | Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB, sm_70, 16 384 MiB, driver 580.178.04 |
| Host | Ubuntu 24.04, kernel 6.8.0-151-generic, Python 3.12.3 |
| Toolkit | CUDA 12.9 (`/home/hermes/cuda-12.9`) |
| Engine | `chatterbox-tts` 0.1.7, `torch` 2.6.0+cu124, `transformers` 5.2.0, `diffusers` 0.29.0, `setuptools<81` |
| Weights | `ResembleAI/chatterbox`: `t3_mtl23ls_v2.safetensors` 2 143 989 752 B, `s3gen.pt` 1 057 165 844 B, `ve.pt`, `conds.pt`, `grapheme_mtl_merged_expanded_v1.json` |
| Vocoder | bundled S3Gen, 24 kHz output |
| ASR for WER | `faster-whisper` large-v3 (ctranslate2, float16), language forced to Russian |

Inside the harness: `ChatterboxMultilingualTTS.from_local("/srv/media/tts/chatterbox", device="cuda")`, then
`model.generate(text, language_id="ru", exaggeration=0.5, cfg_weight=0.5)` with library defaults elsewhere
(`temperature` 0.8, `repetition_penalty` 2.0, `min_p` 0.05, `max_new_tokens` 1000 hard-coded in 0.1.7).

Long forms are where the engine breaks. One call on a 1 142-character script stops at exactly 40.0 s of
audio: the library hard-codes `max_new_tokens=1000`, and at 25 tokens per second of audio that is a 40-second
ceiling. Only 83 of 183 words were voiced, and the tail degenerates into vowel noise, WER 0.88. Splitting the
same script on sentence boundaries into five chunks of 146-278 characters and concatenating with 120 ms of
silence gives a 60.12 s read in 53.54 s of synthesis, RTF 1.12, WER 0.077, 181 of 183 words, peak VRAM
10 619 MiB (chunk buffers stay alive, roughly 1.5x the single-paragraph peak).

Baseline from the earlier Silero v5 run on the same card: 146 characters became 9.65 s of audio in 4.7 s of
synthesis, RTF 2.05. Chatterbox is therefore about 2.2x slower than Silero per second of audio, and on the
V100 a one-minute read costs a bit more than one minute of GPU time.

**Repeats.** n = 3 on the medium phrase: 21.41 s (cold, initializes the watermarker and CUDA kernels),
5.10 s, 4.44 s, RTF 1.03 and 1.09 on the two warm runs. Reporting `0.78 ± 0.48` over all three is misleading
- the spread is the cold start, not the model. Every other configuration is a single run.

**Verdict.** Mixed: Chatterbox Multilingual speaks Russian intelligibly (WER 2-11 % on a 92-word paragraph,
0 % on a short phrase), carries emotion through `exaggeration`, and clones the reference voice on a 3.5 GB
footprint, but it runs at about real time (RTF 1.03-1.12) versus 2.05 for Silero, and it cannot synthesize
anything longer than 40 seconds in one call - a minute-long ad needs sentence chunking, which works
(60 s in 53.5 s, WER 7.7 %).

**Result type.** Mixed.

**Does not show.** Emotion and prosody were not measured objectively: the three `exaggeration` values change
duration (28.0 / 30.1 / 30.0 s) and require a listening test, which was not done. WER is measured with one
ASR model on one rig, so part of the 2-11 % error belongs to the recognizer, not the synthesizer. Stability
beyond one minute (the card asked for 2-3 minutes) was not run: the chunked pipeline is the only path there
and it was exercised for 60 s. VibeVoice-1.5B, the second engine named in the card, was not tested - its
weights are English/Chinese oriented and the Russian check would fail first. Multi-speaker dialogue in one
file was not exercised.
