# V100-36 — Video without RAM ceiling: clip length and 720p

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 96 GB RAM, driver 580._

**Hypothesis.** The VM ran with 32 GB RAM and according to Proxmox was at 99% limit for a day. LTX hit the ceiling at 12 seconds and 177 on the clip count, Wan fell into OOM - part of these limits were set by offload and latencies in RAM, not 16 GB VRAM. Now the VM has 96 GB, cache is free.

**What is needed.** VM 200 (96 GB), pipelines LTX and Wan, same scenario 768×512/97 frames, then 720p and length 15/20/30 s.

**How it was verified.** Clip length until failure, time for 12/20/30 seconds, VRAM and process RSS. Comparison to 177 seconds in 12 seconds. Criterion: clip ≥20 seconds or 720p without offload.

**Risks.** The limit may be in VRAM (16 GB) and in fp16 attention without flow accumulation - then the numbers won't shift, and this is also a result: so focus on the card.

**Result.** RAM was not the limit: process RSS of 41.6 GB at all lengths, VRAM 12.7-13.0 GB. 480p (20 steps): 97/145/193 frames ok - 176/283/416 sec; 241 and 321 - OOM. 720p without tiling fails during decoding after 394 sec (requires 1.72 GB, free 1.61); with VAE tiling, 97 frames take 594 sec at peak 13.55 GB, while 145 frames again OOM. Conclusion: the limit is a 16 GB card, not RAM; without tricks, 12 sec at 480p and 6 sec at 720p.
