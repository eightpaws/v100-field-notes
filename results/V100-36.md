# V100-36 — Video without RAM ceiling: clip length and 720p

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 96 GB RAM, driver 580._

**Hypothesis.** The VM ran with 32 GB RAM and according to Proxmox was at 99% limit for a day. LTX hit the ceiling at 12 seconds and 177 on the clip count, Wan fell into OOM - part of these limits were set by offload and latencies in RAM, not the 16 GB VRAM. Now the VM has 96 GB, cache is free.

**Test rig.** Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB (cc 7.0, 16384 MiB) · driver 580.178.04 · Ubuntu 24.04.5, kernel 6.8.0-151 · EPYC 7282, 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM · Python 3.12.3 · ollama: KV_CACHE_TYPE=q8_0, NUM_PARALLEL=4, KEEP_ALIVE=30m, MAX_LOADED_MODELS=1

**Commands.** Video pipeline (Wan/ACE) 1280x704, 20 steps, VAE tiling on/off; lengths 97 / 145 / 193 / 241 / 321 frames; separately 480p.

**Artifacts.** `[raw/runs/V100-36.json](../raw/runs/V100-36.json)`, `[raw/runs/V100-36-720p.json](../raw/runs/V100-36-720p.json)`, `[raw/runs/V100-36-720p-tiled.json](../raw/runs/V100-36-720p-tiled.json)`

**Repeats.** single run on configuration

**What is needed.** VM 200 (96 GB), pipelines LTX and Wan, same scenario 768×512/97 frames, then 720p and length 15/20/30 s.

**How it was verified.** Clip length until failure, time for 12/20/30 seconds, VRAM and process RSS. Comparison to 177 seconds in 12 seconds. Criterion: clip ≥20 seconds or 720p without offload.

**Risks.** The ceiling can be in VRAM (16 GB) and in fp16 attention without flow accumulation - then the numbers will not shift, and this is also a result: means focus on the card.

**Result.** RAM was not the limit: process RSS of 41.6 GB at all lengths, VRAM 12.7-13.0 GB. 480p (20 steps): 97/145/193 frames ok - 176/283/416 sec; 241 and 321 - OOM. 720p without tiling crashes during decoding after 394 sec (requires 1.72 GB, free 1.61); with VAE tiling, 97 frames take 594 sec at peak 13.55 GB, and 145 frames again OOM. Conclusion: the limit is a 16 GB card, not RAM; without tricks, 12 sec at 480p and 6 sec at 720p.
