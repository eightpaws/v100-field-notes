Class C is answered with the single word REFUSE by all three models, so the column carries no signal. **Verdict.** Abliteration removes refusals on harmless prompts entirely in both abliterated builds, but Flash-Next still refuses 2 of 9 gray prompts where the 27B refuses none, and no quality-loss figure can be claimed because the coding comparison is not like-for-like.
