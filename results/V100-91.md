# V100-91 — Recheck sm70-attn with head_dim 256

_Measured on a Tesla V100-SXM2 16 GB (sm_70) inside a Proxmox VM: 24 vCPU, 94 GiB RAM, driver 580.178.04._

**Question.** The V100-30 card rejected `sm70-attn` due to the criterion "we don't have a model with head_dim 256". The situation has changed: upstream accepted the same FA config for sm_70 (#27997), and on V100, 27B-models with a head of 256 have been measured (101 tok/s per pair of cards). Additionally, there is SplitKV3, q4_0 KV, and DFlash2 in the fork.

**Environment.** Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB, sm_70, driver 580.178.04, CUDA 12.9 (nvcc 12.9), fork fishlikeX/sm70-attn HEAD 5e11845, our build llama.cpp dbaf582 (build 400), model Qwen3.8-27B-GSQ-RCO-IQ3_S.gguf (11.77 GB, 3.4 bpw, 26.9 billion), llama-bench, ub 2048.

**Repeats.** Single run, n=1

**Result.** Yes, the D256 core is faster than our build: a fork of fishlikeX/sm70-attn (5e11845) on Qwen3.8-27B IQ3_S yields **1279.4** tok/s prefill at 8k and **1224.6** at 16k, compared to **1178.9** and **1100.9** in our dbaf582 build (same -fa on, q4_0 KV, V100 16 GB), an increase of +8.5% and +11.2%; decoding is the same (35.9 vs 36.3 tok/s). The core's contribution alone (same binary, LLAMA_SM70_D256=0/1) is more modest: +4.2% at 8k and +8.6% at 16k, with the README-claimed +34-48% applicable to 100-174k context and not reproducible at 8-16k. At f16 KV 16k, neither fork nor our build can run (OOM), so no comparison is possible. Quality was not measured.
